About Us

Compassionate Healthcare, Rooted in Limuru

Limuru Nursing Home Ltd — known locally as Kwa Patel — is a middle-sized hospital in the heart of Limuru Town, directly opposite Naivas Supermarket. Since 1984 we've been a trusted name in community healthcare, combining experienced medical staff with a warm, personal approach to every patient who walks through our doors.

  • 24/7 Emergency CareRound-the-clock medical support, every day of the year.
  • Experienced Medical TeamQualified doctors, surgeons, nurses and specialists across 8 core service areas.
  • SHA / SHIF & Insurance AccreditedAccepted schemes include POMSF, teachers' medical schemes, corporate covers, cash and M-Pesa.
  • Trusted by the Community9,500+ Facebook followers and generations of families who call us their local hospital.
