About Us

Limuru Nursing Home Ltd (Kwa Patel) is a trusted, middle-sized hospital located in the heart of Limuru Town, directly opposite Naivas Supermarket. For over 40 years we have served the Limuru community and surrounding areas with compassionate, quality healthcare — and we're proud to be followed by more than 9,500 people on Facebook.
