Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-in patients?
Yes, we accept walk-in patients at all times. For non-emergency cases we recommend calling ahead to reduce waiting time.
What are your visiting hours?
General visiting hours are 10am–12pm and 5pm–7pm daily. For ICU and maternity wards, please confirm with nursing staff.
Is emergency care available at night?
Yes. Our emergency department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including all public holidays.
Do you have a pharmacy on site?
Yes, our in-house pharmacy is open around the clock to dispense prescriptions and over-the-counter medications.
